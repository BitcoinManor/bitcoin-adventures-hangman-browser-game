# bitcoin-adventures-hangman-browser-game
A kid-friendly Bitcoin word-guessing game starring the Bitcoin Adventures characters. Learn Bitcoin words while you play in the browser!

## How to play

Open `index.html` in your browser. Guess the hidden Bitcoin word one letter at a time by typing or clicking the letter keys. Every wrong guess makes a coin roll away. Find the word before all 6 coins are gone, and a Bitcoin Adventures character will teach you what it means!
